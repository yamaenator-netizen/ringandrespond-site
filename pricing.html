On both plans, Sunny does not book into your practice's schedule and never touches your patient database. Your team calls back — in control. Services labeled coming on the homepage (text-back, confirmations, Open Dental booking, eligibility verification, the billing portal) are not part of either plan today and are never charged for before they exist.

Which one fits? If your days are handled and the leak is nights and weekends, start with After-Hours Capture. If the second line rings at the counter and lunch goes uncovered, 24/7 Complete is the one built for that day. You can move between plans month to month.

  • The pilot is the real plan, for its first 30 days — not a reduced trial version.
