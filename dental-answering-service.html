The question that decides it: do you want calls relayed or resolved? A message slip at 8 AM is a patient who waited 12 hours. A booked appointment at 8 PM is a patient who never waited at all.

7 questions to ask every vendor

  1. What is my all-in cost at my call volume? Get per-minute rates, after-hours surcharges, and holiday fees in writing — not just the base plan price.
  2. Do you book appointments or just take messages? This is the single biggest functional difference between vendors.
  3. How are emergencies escalated? Script-and-page, or real triage against my protocol? Who decides what's urgent?
  4. Will you sign a Business Associate Agreement? Any service handling patient calls needs a BAA. If they won't sign one, walk away.
  5. What are the contract terms? Month-to-month or annual lock-in? What does cancellation actually require?
  6. Can I trial it before committing? You wouldn't hire a receptionist without an interview. Don't buy a service without a pilot.
  7. What happens when you can't handle a call? Vague message slip, or a detailed request with name, number, and reason?

Dental answering service FAQs

What does a dental answering service do?

A dental answering service picks up your calls when your team can't — after hours, weekends, lunch, and overflow. Traditional services have human operators who take messages; AI-based services like Ring & Respond conduct the call: booking appointments, answering questions, and routing emergencies per your rules.

How much does a dental answering service cost?

Traditional services run $200–$600/month plus per-minute charges ($0.80–$1.50+/min). AI receptionists range from about $149 to $1,299/month. Our full pricing breakdown is on our dental answering service cost guide. Ring & Respond is $499 setup + $299/month flat, no per-minute fees.

Traditional answering service vs AI receptionist — which is better for dentists?

It depends on what you want the call to accomplish. Traditional services take messages for morning callbacks; AI receptionists resolve calls live — booking appointments and triaging emergencies. Our full AI receptionist vs answering service comparison covers the tradeoffs honestly.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your existing office number forwards to the service whenever you choose — after hours, lunch, or 24/7. Patients dial the same number they've always dialed.

Can an answering service book dental appointments?

Traditional services generally take messages; your team books in the morning. AI services like Sunny book live on the call using your scheduling rules — providers, emergency slots, new vs. existing patients.

How do answering services handle dental emergencies?

Traditional operators follow a script and page your on-call. Sunny triages against your written protocol — pain, swelling, trauma, bleeding — and bridges true emergencies to your on-call number immediately, without waking you for non-urgent calls.

What should I ask before signing with an answering service?

Ask: (1) all-in cost at my call volume, including per-minute charges; (2) after-hours and holiday surcharges; (3) do you book appointments or just take messages; (4) how are emergencies escalated; (5) will you sign a BAA; (6) contract length and cancellation terms; (7) can I trial it before committing.

What's the difference between a dental phone answering service and a general call center?

A dental phone answering service is built around dental calls: appointment requests, tooth pain, insurance questions, and practice-specific hours and services. A general call center reads a generic script for any business, which is why callers so often end up as a message slip instead of a captured patient.

Is a dental answering service available 24/7?

It should be — missed calls don't keep office hours. Ring & Respond covers after-hours, weekends, holidays, and daytime overflow in one flat rate, so there's no separate 24/7 tier to buy.

How are calls documented for my front desk?

Every call produces a summary your team can act on: caller name, callback number, reason for the call, and the appointment window requested. Your staff confirm or adjust the appointment in your own system — Sunny captures the request, your office keeps control of the schedule.

Can a dental answering service answer in my practice's name?

Yes. Sunny greets callers with your practice's name and uses your approved answers about hours, services, and policies. Callers hear a consistent front-desk experience, not a third-party call center.
