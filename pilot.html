Honest FAQ

Does Sunny book appointments directly into our software?

No — and we won't pretend otherwise. Sunny captures each caller's request and preferred time, and your team confirms it in the morning, so your real schedule stays accurate and under your control. Nothing is ever double-booked by a robot.

What happens in a real dental emergency?

Sunny recognizes urgent situations — severe pain, knocked-out teeth, swelling with fever, trouble breathing or swallowing. True emergencies are directed to call 911 or urgent care immediately, and urgent cases are flagged for your first callback of the morning, with office-approved comfort guidance in the meantime.

Do we need a new phone number?

No. Your patients keep calling the number they know. During setup we arrange forwarding so calls reach Sunny whenever your team can't answer — after hours on the After-Hours plan, or whenever you're tied up on 24/7 Complete.

What does Sunny collect from callers?

Only what's needed for a good callback: name, phone number, whether they're a new or returning patient, why they called, and when they'd like to come in. She never asks for insurance IDs, Social Security numbers, or payment details.

What if Sunny doesn't know an answer?

She says so, kindly and honestly, takes the question down, and adds it to your morning list. She never guesses about prices or policies.

What if we want to cancel?

Cancel any time. Plans are month-to-month, there's no exit fee, and during the 30-day pilot the money-back guarantee means the first month is refunded if Sunny isn't a fit.
