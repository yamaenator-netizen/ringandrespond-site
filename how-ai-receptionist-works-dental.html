A call, step by step

  1. The phone rings. Your office number forwards to Sunny — after hours, on overflow, or 24/7, your choice. Standard call forwarding; no new number, no new hardware.
  2. She answers on the second ring, in your practice's name: "Hi, thanks for calling Your Dental Practice — I'm Sunny, the after-hours assistant..."
  3. She listens and understands. New patient? Toothache? Reschedule? Insurance question? She identifies the need the way a trained receptionist would.
  4. She acts. Routine booking? Scheduled live, per your rules. Emergency? Triaged against your protocol, bridged to on-call if urgent. Question? Answered from your approved answers.
  5. You get the summary. Every call logged — who called, what they needed, what was booked or escalated. Morning brings a clean report, not a voicemail backlog.

The best way to understand it is to experience it: call (619) 202-1085 and have a conversation. That's not a demo recording — it's Sunny, live.

Setup: what we need from you

  • Your hours — when Sunny covers vs. your team (nights, weekends, lunch, overflow, or all of it).
  • Your services — what you offer, so she answers accurately.
  • Scheduling rules — new-patient blocks, providers, emergency slots.
  • Emergency protocol — what counts as urgent, who gets paged, on what number.
  • Your "never" list — anything she should never say, promise, or discuss.
  • Approved answers — insurance plans, pricing guidance, location details.

That's a 5-minute onboarding questionnaire. Most practices are live within days — then it's one call-forwarding setting with your carrier.

What she won't do (on purpose)

Sunny doesn't give clinical advice, diagnose, or prescribe. She doesn't negotiate billing disputes or counsel distressed patients. When a call goes beyond her scope, she captures everything and routes it to your team with full context — or escalates immediately if it's urgent. Knowing what she doesn't know is a feature, not a gap. (For the compliance side, see HIPAA and AI receptionists.)

How it works FAQs

How does an AI receptionist work for a dental office?

Your office number forwards to Sunny when you choose — after hours, overflow, or 24/7. She answers in your practice's name, understands the caller's request, books appointments per your rules, answers routine questions from your approved answers, triages emergencies against your protocol, and sends you a summary of every call.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your existing number forwards to Sunny — a standard carrier feature that takes minutes to set up. Patients dial the same number they've always used.

How long does setup take?

Most practices go live within days. Onboarding is a 5-minute questionnaire: hours, services, new-patient policy, emergency rules, scheduling preferences, and anything Sunny should never say. Then you forward your line.

How does Sunny book appointments?

Using your scheduling rules — which providers take new patients, where new-patient blocks go, how emergency slots work. She collects the details your team needs (name, number, insurance, reason for visit) so every booking arrives complete.

What happens when Sunny can't handle a call?

She doesn't guess. Anything outside her scope — clinical questions, billing disputes, upset callers — becomes a detailed request with full context for your team, or an immediate escalation if it's urgent.

Can I change what Sunny says?

Yes. Your answers to hours, services, insurance, pricing guidance, and emergency rules are all configurable, and updates apply to future calls. Tell us what to change and it's done.

Does it work with my practice management software?

Sunny works from the scheduling rules you define, so she doesn't require PMS integration to start booking correctly on day one. Tell us which software you run during onboarding and we'll configure the workflow around it.

What can't an AI receptionist do?

She won't give clinical advice, diagnose, prescribe, negotiate billing disputes, or counsel distressed patients — those go to your team with full context. She's built to know what she doesn't know.

How do I forward my office phones to Sunny after hours?

It's a setting on your existing phone system: forward unanswered or after-hours calls to Sunny on a schedule, or toggle forwarding when you close. Your number doesn't change, daytime calls ring your team as normal, and we test the routing with you before go-live.

What training does Sunny need about my practice?

Your hours, services, address, insurance approach, and the answers you approve to common questions — collected in a short setup, in your words. If a caller asks something outside the approved answers, Sunny captures the question for your team rather than inventing an answer.

Can I hear Sunny before we set anything up?

Yes — call (619) 202-1085 any time and put her through a normal patient call. Most owners test her with a made-up emergency first, which is exactly the call that matters at 9 PM.

What happens if our office changes hours or services later?

Tell us and the approved answers are updated — holiday hours, a new hygienist, a service you no longer offer. Your team isn't editing software; you send the change and it's reflected in what Sunny says.
