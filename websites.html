The Standard

What every build gets

  • Loads in about a second. No heavy page builders, no waiting, no spinning wheels.
  • Perfect accessibility scores. Big tap targets, honest contrast, and a site that works with a keyboard or a screen reader.
  • Your own real photos — never stock pictures pretending to be your shop, your crew, or your work.
  • No fake claims and no invented reviews. If it isn't true, it isn't on the site.
  • Your phone and your booking up front. The number is never buried three taps deep.
  • English and Spanish, side by side — and checked daily after launch, the same way we check this one.
