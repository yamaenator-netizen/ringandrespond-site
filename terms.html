2. What Sunny does

Sunny is an AI receptionist that answers your practice's phone line in your practice's name, in English, Spanish, Arabic, and Farsi. On each call she:

  • Greets the caller and discloses that the call is recorded.
  • Collects the caller's name, a callback number (read back to confirm it), the reason for the call in the caller's own words, and a preferred callback time.
  • Builds a callback list your team works from, flags urgent callers for priority follow-up, and reports what was captured.

The Sunny reachable at our demo line, (619) 202-1085, and in the example film on this site, is an example configuration. Your Sunny is configured to your office's hours, services, and rules, and you approve test calls before she talks to a real patient.

3. What Sunny does not do

  • Sunny does not book appointments directly into your practice software or calendar. She captures requests; your team confirms appointments in your own system.
  • Sunny gives no medical or dental advice and makes no diagnosis. If a caller describes a true emergency, Sunny directs them to 911 or urgent care. Your practice remains responsible for clinical decisions and for following up its callback list promptly — Sunny is answering support, not a substitute for your team's judgment.
  • Sunny does not send text messages. Any future texting feature will be described and agreed separately.
  • Sunny follows the rules you approve. Like any receptionist — human or AI — she can occasionally misunderstand a caller; the callback list and recordings exist so your team can review and catch what matters.

4. Before any patient calls

Two things happen before Sunny takes a real patient call for your practice: (1) your practice's BAA with us is signed, and (2) you have approved test calls. Until both are done, Sunny handles test traffic only. Your practice is responsible for the accuracy of the office information you give us (hours, services, emergency rules) and for setting up call forwarding as we instruct.

5. The pilot and the money-back guarantee

  • Guarantee: if Sunny doesn't earn her keep in the first 30 days, email hello@ringandrespond.com within the 30 days and we refund the full first month. Unconditional — no forms, no exit interview, no questions about why.
  • Around day 25 you receive a capture report: every call Sunny caught, what callers asked for, and what your team confirmed.
  • If you don't ask for the refund, the plan simply continues under Section 6.
