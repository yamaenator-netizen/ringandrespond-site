The hidden fees to watch for

  • Per-minute overages. A "$200/month" plan at $1.50/minute becomes $500+ in a busy month. Ask for the all-in cost at your call volume.

  • Booking fees. Some services charge per appointment booked on top of the base plan.
  • Setup and onboarding fees. $500–$3,500 is common, sometimes buried in the contract.
