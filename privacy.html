Privacy Policy

What Ring & Respond collects, why, and what we never do with it — in plain language.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Who we are

Yama Abbassi, doing business as Ring & Respond ("we," "us") operates ringandrespond.com and Sunny, an AI receptionist service for dental practices. Our mailing address is 8441 Buckland St, La Mesa, CA 91942, and our privacy contact is hello@ringandrespond.com.

This policy covers two different things, and we keep them separate:

  • This website — the pages, the site chat, the setup form, and the demo line, used by dental practices considering Sunny.
  • The Sunny service — the calls Sunny answers on behalf of a practice once that practice is a customer. On those calls we act as the practice's service provider (under HIPAA, its business associate), and caller information belongs to the practice.

2. What we collect on this website

When you ask us to call you back

If you use the "Have Yama call me" option in the site chat, we collect your name, phone number, and the best time to reach you. Those three things are emailed to our team so we can call you. Nothing else in your chat conversation is sent to us — the chat's question-and-answer matching happens in your own browser.

When you use the guided signup in the chat

The chat's optional signup walkthrough asks for your practice name, city and state, a contact name, an email address, a callback number, and the call coverage you want. Those answers are not sent to us. They are kept only in your own browser (in local storage, so you can pick up where you left off) and are cleared when you finish or start over. Checkout itself happens on Stripe's site — see "Payments" below.

When you complete the setup form after paying

The setup form at /onboard collects office information only: practice name and address, office phone, office hours, services offered, whether you accept new patients, how you want calls handled, an optional on-call number for urgent callers, your practice software, and an office email. The form says so up front, and we repeat it here: please never enter patient names, health information, or appointment details into any form or chat on this website. Your setup answers are emailed to our team so we can configure your Sunny.

When you contact us directly

If you email or call us, we receive your email address or phone number and whatever you include in your message.

Technical information

Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically processes standard request information — such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested — to serve the site and keep it secure. Our forms use IP addresses briefly, in memory, to limit abuse (for example, repeated submissions); we do not build profiles from this. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the technical request needed to deliver the font files. This website does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout pages. Your card number and billing details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. Stripe shares with us the information we need to run your account — such as the name and email on the payment and whether a payment succeeded — under Stripe's own privacy policy.

4. What we collect through the Sunny service

Once a practice is a customer and its business associate agreement (BAA) with us is signed, Sunny answers that practice's calls. On those calls, Sunny collects what a receptionist would: the caller's name, a callback number (read back to confirm it), the reason for the call in the caller's own words, and a preferred callback time. Calls are recorded and transcribed so the practice can review them; the recording is disclosed at the start of every call, as California law requires.

That information is assembled into a callback list for the practice's team. We handle caller information only to provide the service to that practice — it is the practice's information, not ours to use for anything else. We never sell personal information, we never use patient or call information to train AI models, and no real patient calls are handled before the practice's BAA is in place. Call handling runs through voice-service providers that are under a BAA with us before any patient calls reach them.

5. How we use information

  • To call you back or reply when you ask us to.
  • To set up, configure, and operate Sunny for your practice.
  • To deliver callback lists and the pilot capture report to your team.
  • To bill you and manage your subscription (through Stripe).
  • To keep the website and service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use website visitor information for advertising, and we do not sell or "share" personal information as those words are defined under California privacy law.

6. Who information goes to

Only the service providers needed to run what you used, and only for that purpose:

  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Resend — delivers the form and notification emails to our inbox.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Google — our business email inbox, and font delivery on this site.
  • Voice-service providers under a BAA — for customer call handling only, as described in Section 4.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it (for example, a valid court order), or to protect the rights and safety of our customers and callers.

7. How long we keep it

Callback requests and setup submissions are delivered to our business email inbox and kept for as long as we need them to respond, set up your service, and keep ordinary business records. Sunny call recordings, transcripts, and callback lists are kept according to the practice's agreement with us. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see Section 8.

8. Your choices and California rights

California residents may ask us to tell them what personal information we hold about them, and may ask us to correct or delete it. We do not sell personal information, so there is no sale to opt out of. We will never discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email hello@ringandrespond.com and we will respond within the time California law allows. If your request concerns a call you made to a dental practice that uses Sunny, contact the practice as well — they are the covered entity for that call, and we will route and support your request through them.

9. Security

Information sent through this website travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), form submissions are rate-limited against abuse, and access to customer information is limited to our team. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat a dental practice's trust — and its patients' information — as the core of this business.

10. Who this site is for

This website and service are for dental practices and their staff — businesses, not consumers, and not children. Please do not use the website's chat or forms for patient information of any kind.

11. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its "last updated" date, and for material changes affecting customers we will notify practices by email.
